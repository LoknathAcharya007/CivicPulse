## Limitations
- Synthetic records; priority labels are simulated expert decisions, not government records.
- Streetlight, park landscaping, drain desilting and leak-repair rates are assumptions; package quantities are engineering assumptions; excavation and dismantling are not included.
- No regional Building Cost Index or price-rise adjustment applied; the July 2026 construction wage rate is unavailable (not needed for composite-rate costing).
- One set of percentage effects is shared by all categories in the linear model.
